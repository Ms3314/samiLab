## What it is

KnowmeBetter is an anonymous messaging app. You create a profile with a unique link, share it, and people send you messages and questions without revealing who they are.

## Features

- **Anonymous messages.** Senders never reveal their identity.
- **Profile links.** Share your own link, such as /u/username, to receive messages.
- **Dashboard.** Read your messages and turn message acceptance on or off.
- **Accounts.** Sign in with email or username and password, and verify your account with a code sent to your email.
- **Copy link.** One click copies your link to share it.
